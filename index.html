Voice Commands Guide
WhatsApp:
• "open whatsapp"
• "open whatsapp and text 'message' to contact"
• "open whatsapp and call contact"
• "open whatsapp and video call contact"
• "call [contact]"
• "video call [contact]"
Volume Control:
• "volume up" / "increase volume"
• "volume down" / "decrease volume"
• "mute" / "unmute"
• "set volume to [number] percent"
• "what's the volume" / "current volume"
Brightness Control:
• "brightness up" / "increase brightness"
• "brightness down" / "decrease brightness"
• "set brightness to [number] percent"
• "what's the brightness" / "current brightness"
YouTube:
• "play [song/video name] on youtube"
• "search for [query] on youtube"
• "play [song/video name]"
Other:
• "help" (shows command list)
• "stop listening" / "exit" / "quit"

This assistant is designed for accessibility. If you need help, just say "Help" or "What can you do?"
